## 1. Canvas and composition

Use a 3:4 portrait canvas. The standard final raster is 1440 × 1920.

Reserve approximate vertical zones:

- Top 5–45%: distinctive hero ingredients.
- Middle 40–75%: structural ingredients and the base explosion.
- Bottom 72–96%: product and white porcelain plate.
- Outer 4–22% on each side: alternating labels and leader lines.

These percentages are guidelines. Preserve food readability and side negative space before enforcing symmetry.

Use pure black as a seamless background. Avoid visible floors, gradients, smoke, decorative patterns, props, packaging, tables, hands, or utensils unless explicitly requested.

## 2. Product identity

Preserve these invariants from the supplied references:

- Overall silhouette and diameter-to-height ratio.
- Edge treatment: scalloped, fluted, smooth, folded, or laminated.
- Top surface: embossed pattern, punctures, central mark, glaze, or browning.
- Crust color, thickness, crumb, and gloss.
- Filling colors and outside-to-inside order.
- Characteristic cut or serving style.

Use a slightly elevated 45-degree camera angle for most round pastries. Keep the product large enough to read at phone size.

For a cutaway, remove approximately one quarter and place the wedge close to the remaining product. Aim the cut face at the camera. Avoid a second unrelated whole product.

## 3. Ingredient layer design

Every ingredient layer needs:

- Its own vertical band.
- A clean black gap from adjacent layers.
- A recognizable central mass.
- Several larger pieces rotated at different angles.
- Smaller fragments or particles traveling outward.
- Foreground/background scale variation.

Do not make layers look like flat grocery piles. Freeze an explosion in motion.

Make the base explosion the largest event when it contains flour, sugar, and oil:

- Flour: white powder towers, dust plumes, soft airborne granules.
- Sugar: faceted, translucent, sparkling medium-size crystals.
- Liquid oil: glossy warm-gold ribbons, arcs, beads, and suspended droplets.

Keep all three readable inside one shared explosion without blending them into an undefined beige cloud.

## 4. Material vocabulary

Use these descriptions to prevent common image-model substitutions:

| Ingredient | Required visual language | Avoid |
|---|---|---|
| 海鸭蛋黄 | vivid orange salted yolk spheres; some cleanly broken; dense crumbly oily interior | egg whites, shells, fried eggs |
| 红枣泥 | dark red-brown jujube paste; glossy thick ribbons and torn dollops; subtle date fiber | chocolate, red bean paste unless requested |
| 雪媚娘 | milk-white glutinous-rice mochi sheets, rounded pieces, elastic ribbons, light starch dust | cheese, cream, marshmallow strings |
| 黑芝麻 | small glossy black teardrop seeds with scattered highlights | black beans, pepper |
| 白芝麻 | small warm-ivory teardrop seeds | rice grains |
| 花生 | peeled roasted peanut kernels, split halves, warm tan surface | almonds or cashews |
| 青花椒 | bright fresh green split husks with citrus-like lobes | green peas |
| 藤椒 | darker olive-green peppercorn clusters attached to thin stems | identical bright-green Qing peppercorns |
| 小茴香 | elongated green-brown ridged fennel seeds | cumin unless requested |
| 盐 | coarse opaque or translucent mineral crystals | sugar-like sparkling candy |
| 中粒白砂糖 | clean faceted medium white crystals, bright sparkle | powdered sugar or salt chunks |
| 五星面粉 | fine white wheat flour, dramatic powder cloud | dough mass |
| 液态油 | transparent warm-gold edible oil ribbons and droplets | honey, caramel, dark syrup |

Describe other ingredients by shape, size, color, surface, internal texture, and physical behavior.

## 5. Motion and lighting

Use motion cues together:

- radial fragment trajectories;
- rotation and tilted pieces;
- overlapping foreground pieces;
- tiny particles between large forms;
- several pieces moving toward camera;
- several pieces receding into black;
- irregular edges rather than perfect flat rows.

Light with a warm key/rim combination. Preserve deep black gaps. Use highlights to separate oil, sugar, paste, yolk, crust, and porcelain. Avoid overexposure that turns every white ingredient into the same material.

## 6. Typography safe zones

Generate with empty side margins. Keep central food inside roughly the middle 60–72% of the width.

At 1440 × 1920, use these starting values:

- Ingredient name: 40–44 px CJK font.
- Flavor note: 22–25 px CJK font.
- Name color: warm ivory.
- Note color: muted warm gold.
- Leader line: 3 px warm gold.
- Endpoint: 14 px outer gold dot with a dark 6 px center.
- Horizontal margin: 60–70 px.

Alternate labels left and right to create rhythm. Keep leader lines short and point to unmistakable material, not empty space.

## 7. Series consistency

Across a product series, keep stable:

- 3:4 canvas and 1440 × 1920 export.
- Black background and white plate.
- Gold/ivory typography.
- Label hierarchy and note size.
- Base explosion treatment.
- Camera angle and food scale.

Allow the hero ingredient colors, layer count, cutaway, and product surface to change per product.
