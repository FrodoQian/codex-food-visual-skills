## 1. How to read the examples

These examples document composition decisions refined through real poster iterations. They are not recipe specifications and do not claim ingredient percentages.

For a new product:

1. Preserve the new product's own references.
2. Use the new user's exact ingredient names.
3. Re-plan the layer count and material appearance.
4. Reuse only the visual workflow, typography, and QA method.

## 2. Example A: 赵州饼

### User-style request

```text
把赵州饼放在白瓷盘里，原料从底部向上爆炸开。
配料有面粉、油、白砂糖、黑芝麻、白芝麻、花生、葡萄干、
猕猴桃干、蔓越莓干、巴旦木和腰果。每种原料分别标注名字。
```

### Product decision

Use one recognizable round Zhao Zhou pastry on a white porcelain plate. Preserve the golden baked surface, characteristic central red mark, punctures, thickness, and round silhouette.

If no cross-section reference is supplied, keep the product whole. Do not invent an internal cutaway.

### Visual grouping

The ingredient list contains three groups:

- Base ingredients: flour, sugar, liquid oil.
- Seeds and nuts: black sesame, white sesame, peanuts, almonds, cashews.
- Dried fruits: raisins, dried kiwi, dried cranberries.

### Recommended top-to-bottom order

```text
腰果
巴旦木
蔓越莓干
猕猴桃干
葡萄干
花生
白芝麻
黑芝麻
五星面粉＋中粒白砂糖＋液态油大型爆炸云
赵州饼＋白瓷盘
```

The order creates alternating color and scale: large pale nuts, red and green fruit, dark raisins, warm peanuts, light sesame, dark sesame, then the bright base cloud.

### Label strategy

Use only names for a clean ingredient catalog, or add notes when the series has already adopted a two-line label system.

Possible safe notes:

```text
腰果：香醇酥脆
巴旦木：坚果醇香
蔓越莓干：酸甜明亮
猕猴桃干：清甜果香
葡萄干：自然甜润
花生：酥香饱满
白芝麻：清香细腻
黑芝麻：浓郁醇香
五星面粉：构筑酥松饼皮
中粒白砂糖：甜度柔和 · 入口酥化
液态油：润泽起酥
```

Use these as editable sensory copy, not as mandatory facts.

### QA focus

- Cashews and almonds must not be mixed into one generic nut layer.
- Raisins, dried kiwi, and dried cranberries need distinct dark, green, and red color identities.
- Black and white sesame need separate bands and separate labels.
- The finished product must remain the supplied Zhao Zhou pastry, not a generic nut energy bar.

### Reusable lesson

For a long ingredient list, use color rhythm and ingredient scale to keep the poster readable. Never compress all nuts or fruits into one mixed layer just to save vertical space.

## 3. Example B: 桃酥皮椒盐

### User-style request

```text
现在开始拆解桃酥皮椒盐。
成分是液态油、五星面粉、中粒白砂糖、黑芝麻、白芝麻、花生、
青花椒、藤椒、盐和小茴香。分解方式和之前一样。
```

### Product decision

Use one pale-golden round pastry with the supplied embossed top and thick scalloped edge. Keep the slightly crumbly shortbread texture. Place it whole on a white porcelain plate because the surface pattern is the main identity.

### Main challenge

The ingredient list contains several small particles with similar scale:

- black sesame and white sesame;
- Qing peppercorn and vine peppercorn;
- salt and sugar crystals;
- fennel seeds and other spice-like fragments.

The prompt must describe each material by exact color, shape, surface, and structure.

### Recommended top-to-bottom order

```text
小茴香
藤椒
青花椒
盐
花生
白芝麻
黑芝麻
五星面粉＋中粒白砂糖＋液态油大型爆炸云
桃酥皮椒盐＋白瓷盘
```

### Material distinctions

- 小茴香: elongated green-brown ridged seeds.
- 藤椒: darker olive-green clusters attached to thin stems.
- 青花椒: brighter fresh-green split husks.
- 盐: coarse mineral crystals with less candy-like sparkle than sugar.
- 中粒白砂糖: smaller faceted crystals dispersed inside the base cloud.

### Approved two-line label pattern

```text
小茴香｜温润回香
藤椒｜清麻悠长
青花椒｜鲜麻提香
盐｜平衡咸香
花生｜酥香饱满
白芝麻｜清香细腻
黑芝麻｜浓郁醇香
五星面粉｜构筑酥松饼皮
中粒白砂糖｜甜度柔和 · 入口酥化
液态油｜润泽起酥
桃酥皮椒盐｜桃酥酥皮 · 椒盐咸香
```

### QA focus

- Qing peppercorn and vine peppercorn must have different greens and structures.
- Salt must be pointed to separately from the sugar crystals in the base explosion.
- Sesame layers should remain seeds, not coarse beans or pepper grains.
- The whole pastry should be single and centered, without a second pastry or wood table.

### Reusable lesson

When ingredients are visually similar, material accuracy matters more than the number of flying particles. Use explicit negative descriptions such as “not green peas,” “not sugar candy,” and “not cumin.”

## 4. Example C: 四层蛋黄蛋月烧

### User-style request

```text
这是我们的四层蛋黄蛋月烧。
成分是液态油、五星面粉、中粒白砂糖、海鸭蛋黄、红枣泥和雪媚娘。
分解方式和之前一样。
```

### Product decision

Use a quarter-cut composition because the product name and value are tied to its internal structure. Show one remaining pastry plus its removed wedge on one white plate.

Preserve the source product's glossy golden embossed top, thick scalloped side wall, crust color, and proportions.

### Required cutaway order

From outside to inside:

```text
酥皮
雪媚娘
红枣泥
海鸭蛋黄
```

The cut face should turn toward camera and be large enough to read on a phone.

### Recommended top-to-bottom order

```text
海鸭蛋黄
红枣泥
雪媚娘
五星面粉＋中粒白砂糖＋液态油大型爆炸云
四层蛋黄蛋月烧切面＋白瓷盘
```

### Material distinctions

- 海鸭蛋黄: vivid orange salted yolk spheres, with some broken crumbly interiors; no egg whites or shells.
- 红枣泥: dark red-brown glossy jujube paste ribbons with subtle date texture; not chocolate.
- 雪媚娘: milk-white glutinous-rice mochi sheets and elastic ribbons with light starch dust; not cheese or whipped cream.

### Approved two-line label pattern

```text
海鸭蛋黄｜咸香绵密
红枣泥｜枣香浓郁
雪媚娘｜软糯拉丝
五星面粉｜构筑酥松饼皮
中粒白砂糖｜甜度柔和 · 入口酥化
液态油｜润泽起酥
四层蛋黄蛋月烧｜酥皮 · 雪媚娘 · 枣泥 · 海鸭蛋黄
```

### QA focus

- The cutaway must show all four layers in the correct order.
- The removed wedge should belong to the remaining pastry and not look like a second whole product.
- Snow-mochi must not become cheese.
- Jujube paste must not become chocolate.
- Egg yolk should be orange and dense, not a fried or raw egg.

### Reusable lesson

For layered products, the product cutaway and exploded ingredient bands must tell the same story. If the cut face and upper layer order disagree, the poster is not finished.

## 5. Reusable request templates

### Whole-product template

```text
使用 $create-food-ingredient-explosion-poster。
产品名称：[准确名称]
产品照片：[附图]
配料：[准确列表]
保持产品完整，放在白瓷盘里。原料从下向上动态分层爆炸，
3:4纯黑背景，名称和风味说明分别标注，不要外框和层数。
```

### Cutaway template

```text
使用 $create-food-ingredient-explosion-poster。
产品名称：[准确名称]
整体照片和剖面照片：[附图]
配料：[准确列表]
产品切开约四分之一，切面从外到内是：[层次列表]。
上方原料独立爆炸，底部面粉、糖和油形成大型爆炸云。
输出3:4有字版和无字版。
```

### Revision template

```text
继续修改当前成稿，只调整：[需要修改的位置]。
保持产品、白瓷盘、其他原料层、黑色背景、灯光和3:4构图不变。
```

## 6. Lessons across all examples

1. Product identity comes from references, not from generic category knowledge.
2. Ingredient names are exact copy and should not be generated inside the image model.
3. Layer order is a visual narrative and must not imply ingredient percentage.
4. Similar materials need explicit shape, color, texture, and negative constraints.
5. Flour, sugar, and oil need separate label targets even when they share one explosion cloud.
6. Padding to 3:4 is safer than cropping a successful generated composition.
7. One targeted correction is better than regenerating the entire image for a local issue.
8. Deliver both labeled and unlabeled versions so future revisions stay flexible.
